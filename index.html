FAQ

Common questions

How much does a website cost?

Every business is different, so we quote per project. Simple landing pages start small; full redesigns like the ones shown above are scoped after a quick call. You’ll always get a fixed price up front — no surprises.

How long does it take?

Most redesigns launch within 10–14 days. New multi-page builds typically take 2–3 weeks depending on content and feedback rounds.

Do I own my website?

Yes — 100%. You own the code, the content and the domain. We can host it for you or hand everything over; there’s no lock-in.

Can you redesign my existing site?

Absolutely — that’s our specialty. Send us your current URL and we’ll show you exactly what we’d change and why. That’s how the Wayne Kidd and Edelen Doors projects started.

What about after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of free support. After that, our maintenance plans keep your site fast, updated and backed up — or you’re free to manage it yourself.
